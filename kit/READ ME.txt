BUILD WII PARTY - for friends who want to play together online
=============================================================

This kit makes Wii Party for your PC from your own Wii Party disc. The game itself
is not included: you need your own disc, copied to your PC once with Dolphin.

1. Copy your disc to a folder (done once):
   Install the free Dolphin emulator (dolphin-emu.org). Put your disc image in Dolphin,
   right-click Wii Party > Properties > Filesystem > right-click the disc >
   Extract Entire Disc, and pick an empty folder.

2. Unzip this kit to a folder WITHOUT spaces in its name, for example C:\WiiPartyKit

3. Double-click "Build Wii Party.bat" and choose your disc folder when asked.
   It downloads the tools it needs, then builds the game. This takes 10-40 minutes
   and about 6 GB of disc space. Leave the black window open until it says Done.

4. Open the new "Wii Party" folder and double-click "Wii Party.bat".
   To play online: Online tab > type the room code your friend sent you > JOIN.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": press "More info", then "Run anyway".
If something goes wrong, send a screenshot of the black window to whoever helps you.
